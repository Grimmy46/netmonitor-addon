What happened

Starting ~8:14 PM, everything leaving the site degraded together while the local gateway stayed perfect (1 ms, 0% loss) — so this was an upstream internet / WAN event, not a local outage.

Two acute packet-loss bursts hit the fleet: 43 of 54 kiosks lost pings in the same minute at 8:19 PM, and 49 of 54 at 8:33 PM. External DNS climbed from ~4 ms to ~100 ms, and — the part guests felt — the SignaPay payment gateway jumped from ~0.9 s to a peak of 7.8 s and the Funcard kiosk URL from ~0.2 s to 9.3 s, worst between 8:19 and 8:40 PM. During those spikes, card transactions and kiosk page loads would have been timing out.

Now recovered. Loss tapered off after ~8:43 PM; gateway, DNS, payments and kiosks are all back to normal. Nothing in the local network needs fixing — if this recurs, it's one to raise with the site's ISP / uplink provider.
